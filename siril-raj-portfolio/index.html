My Story

I am currently pursuing my B.Tech degree (3rd Year, 1st Semester) at Andhra Loyola Institute of Engineering and Technology (ALIET). My passion lies in understanding how software systems execute beneath the surface—from core memory allocation and OOP paradigms in Java to transaction integrity and normalized schema design in Database Management Systems.

Prior to my engineering journey, I completed my intermediate education at Sri Akshara Junior College, Kanchikacherla, where I honed my fundamental analytical reasoning, mathematical models, and discipline.

The Philosophy of Questioning

Many developers jump straight into coding; I believe exceptional engineering begins by asking the right questions.

  • Why this data structure? Understanding trade-offs between memory, time complexity, and scaling limits.
  • How will the database scale? Eliminating redundant queries, designing clean foreign keys, and indexing hotspots.
  • Where can edge cases fail? Stress-testing boundaries before users ever touch the application.
